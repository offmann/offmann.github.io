How I help

A practical mix of engineering, data, and strategy.

Most AI work fails in the handoff between idea, data, product, and production. I work across that handoff.

LLM Product Engineering

I design and ship LLM applications: RAG chatbots, knowledge assistants, and LLM eval suites tailored to your data and use case. Quick wins that move the needle, from early pilots to 10k-user rollouts.

What this can include
  • Retrieval pipelines (vector, hybrid, graph, search tuning from logs)
  • Evaluation frameworks (LangSmith, custom)
  • Indexing & enrichment workflows
  • Latency & cost optimisation on AWS / Azure

Data Science & MLOps

I turn messy data into structured, usable datasets and build semantic search and recommender systems on top, using LLMs for feature engineering, enrichment, and large-scale dataset creation.

What this can include
  • Data products from unstructured sources (tickets, logs, product feeds, documents)
  • LLM-powered labelling, feature engineering, and enrichment at scale
  • Semantic search and recommenders combining structured and unstructured data
  • ETL pipelines with CI/CD, tests, and drift / performance monitoring

AI Strategy & Advisory

I help teams pick the right AI bets, build a realistic roadmap, align stakeholders, and grow internal capability while staying close enough to the implementation to keep the advice honest.

What this can include
  • ROI and risk workshops grounded in your data and workflows
  • Use-case discovery and prioritisation (what to build first, what to skip)
  • Hiring and tech-stack decisions (build vs buy, vendor selection)
  • Mentoring internal engineers and product teams on shipping with LLMs
